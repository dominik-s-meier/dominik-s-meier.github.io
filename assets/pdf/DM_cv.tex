% Research Interests
\section*{Research Interests}

My research examines individual and institutional philanthropy with a focus on global contexts. At the individual level, I study prosocial behavior including charitable giving and volunteering across cultures and platforms. At the institutional level, I investigate how nonprofits, foundations, and corporate philanthropy programs operate internationally, with particular attention to how nonprofit and for-profit organizations differ and overlap in their missions and contributions to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). I employ big data analytics, machine learning, and natural language processing to analyze philanthropic patterns at scale, including developing algorithms to detect SDG-related content in text.
